one mutation, paired runs, hard quality gates, and weekly keep/discard.
These independent variants remain deferred until v4 has enough verified runs:

1. `passive-observatory` — dashboards and drift alerts over organic runs.
2. `friction-miner` — cluster follow-ups, reroutes, rework, errors, and teacher turns.
3. `shadow-teacher` — blind post-hoc critique that cannot affect the live task.
4. `golden-replay-lab` — curated, sanitized, reproducible repository snapshots.
5. `harness-model-league` — paired Codex/OpenCode/Copilot and MiMo/Luna tournaments.
6. `prompt-ablation-lab` — remove or alter one prompt gene to measure causality.
7. `karpathy-hill-climber` — continuous single-mutation keep/discard experiments.
8. `contextual-bandit` — conservative online recipe selection after sufficient data.
9. `evolutionary-prompt-league` — multi-candidate search when cost is justified.

Prioritize golden replay and the harness/model league, then friction and shadow
analysis. Online bandits or evolutionary search require stable paired evidence.
