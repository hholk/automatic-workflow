# Review

Use after a change exists and needs independent evidence.

Dispatch `aw-glm-review` read-only. Give it the objective, acceptance criteria,
base/diff scope, and verify command, but not the builder's conclusions. It checks
correctness, regressions, permissions, and unexpected scope, and may run safe
verification. Findings require path and evidence; style-only preferences are not
defects. The host resolves material findings and performs final verification.
